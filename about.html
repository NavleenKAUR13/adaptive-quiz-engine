About

Why Quizzy exists

A quiz that remembers what tripped you up — and brings it back.

Our mission

Most quiz apps treat every question the same way, every time. Quizzy doesn't. Every subject is built around the SM-2 spaced-repetition algorithm — the same scheduling idea behind tools like Anki — so a question you get wrong shows up again soon, and a question you've clearly mastered gets out of your way for longer. The goal isn't more questions; it's the right question at the right time.

How the adaptive engine works

Each test pulls 6 questions per subject.
Anything overdue for review comes first.
Wrong answers reset their schedule — they resurface in your very next attempt on that subject.
Correct, fast answers push a question further into the future.

What that means for you

You stop wasting time re-answering things you already know, and stop losing track of the things you don't. Test 1 and Test 2 are free for every subject; Test 3 unlocks with Premium for a deeper, more advanced pass at the same subject.

Built for focused, self-paced learners

Quizzy started as a small HTML/CSS/JavaScript question bank and grew into a subject-wise adaptive platform with dashboards, streaks, achievements and a leaderboard — while keeping the same core scheduling logic from day one. Your progress lives in your account and stays with you between sessions.
